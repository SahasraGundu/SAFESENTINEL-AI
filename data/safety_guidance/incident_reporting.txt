FACTORY SAFETY GUIDANCE — INCIDENT & NEAR-MISS REPORTING
(Demonstration / reference document)

Section 1: Reporting Threshold
Any event that could reasonably have resulted in injury, even if it
did not, must be logged as a Near Miss. Under-reporting near misses
removes the early-warning data safety teams rely on to spot precursor
patterns before an actual incident occurs.

Section 2: Investigation Priority
Locations or hazard types with a rising report frequency over a
rolling 30-day window should be prioritized for investigation ahead
of locations with a flat or declining trend, even if their raw report
count is lower.

Section 3: Escalation
Any location with 3 or more distinct hazard types recurring together
(e.g., leakage + wet floor + missing signage) should be treated as an
elevated precursor signal warranting a site walkthrough, not just a
paperwork review.
