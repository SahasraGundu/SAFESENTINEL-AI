FACTORY SAFETY GUIDANCE — MACHINE GUARDING & INTERLOCKS
(Demonstration / reference document)

Section 1: Interlock Function
Safety interlocks on presses, conveyors, and stamping equipment must
engage on the first attempt. A failure to engage on first attempt,
even if resolved by a manual reset, must be logged as a near-miss and
inspected before the next shift begins.

Section 2: Grinding or Abnormal Noise
Unusual noise from conveyor or motor assemblies preceding a stall is
a common early indicator of bearing or belt failure. Equipment should
be tagged out and inspected rather than restarted repeatedly.

Section 3: Guarding
All moving parts within reach of an operator's normal working position
must have a fixed or interlocked guard in place at all times.
