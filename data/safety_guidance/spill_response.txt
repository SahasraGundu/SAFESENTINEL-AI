FACTORY SAFETY GUIDANCE — SPILL & LEAK RESPONSE
(Demonstration / reference document — not an official regulatory standard)

Section 1: Immediate Response
When a hydraulic, oil, or coolant leak is identified, the area must be
cordoned off immediately using cones or barrier tape. Wet-floor signage
must be placed at both approach points to the spill within 5 minutes
of discovery.

Section 2: Recurring Leaks
If a leak recurs at the same equipment more than twice within a 30-day
period, this is classified as a CHRONIC LEAK and must be escalated to
the maintenance engineering team for root-cause investigation rather
than repeated surface cleanup. Chronic leaks combined with missing
signage are a leading precursor to slip-and-fall incidents.

Section 3: Cleanup
Use absorbent granules for oil-based leaks. Do not use standard mops
for hydraulic fluid, as this spreads a thin film that remains slippery
after visible liquid is removed.

Section 4: Night Shift Considerations
Night shift crews should be provided with dedicated spill-response kits
at each production line, since maintenance response times are
typically longer outside of day-shift hours.
