FACTORY SAFETY GUIDANCE — PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
(Demonstration / reference document)

Section 1: Minimum Requirements
Safety goggles, gloves, and closed-toe footwear are mandatory in all
packaging and assembly areas. High-visibility vests are mandatory for
any personnel working within 3 meters of active forklift traffic.

Section 2: Non-Compliance Patterns
Repeated PPE non-compliance observations in the same area often
indicate a supervision or access gap (e.g., PPE not readily available
at the station) rather than individual negligence. Areas with 3+
non-compliance observations in a month should receive a PPE
availability audit before disciplinary escalation.

Section 3: Forklift Zones
Pedestrians in forklift-active zones must use marked crossings only.
Operators must sound the horn at all blind corners and intersections.
