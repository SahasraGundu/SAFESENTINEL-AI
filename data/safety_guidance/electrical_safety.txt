FACTORY SAFETY GUIDANCE — ELECTRICAL SAFETY
(Demonstration / reference document)

Section 1: Panel Access
Electrical panels and substation enclosures must remain closed and
locked except during authorized maintenance. An open panel with
accessible live components is an immediate stop-work condition for
the surrounding area.

Section 2: Exposed Wiring
Any exposed or visibly worn wiring must be reported and de-energized
at the earliest opportunity. Temporary insulation (tape) is not an
acceptable long-term fix.

Section 3: Lockout/Tagout
All electrical maintenance must follow lockout/tagout procedure.
Panels must be visually verified de-energized before work begins.
